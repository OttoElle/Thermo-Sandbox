Thermo Sandbox – Quick Guide

1. Building & Editing: When the simulation is stopped (■ Stop), click any tool mode button in the top ribbon to draw on the canvas.

2. Properties Inspector: The left panel dynamically shows live sliders and synchronized numeric input boxes for the active tool or selection.

3. Multi-Select & Transforms: Hold Shift and click to select multiple objects. Use TRANSFORM tools to rotate 90° or flip horizontally / vertically.

4. Thermal Triad: Choose between constant-temperature Reservoir, permeable Porous Matrix, or impermeable Solid Thermal Block.

5. Valves & Emitters: Emitters feature 360° emission and can be clicked to toggle ON/OFF. Valves include Pressure Relief Valves (PRV) with automatic release.
