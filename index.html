Thermo Sandbox – Quick Guide

Build. With the simulation stopped, pick a tool in the ribbon and drag on the canvas. Type a number while drawing for an exact size. The panel on the left shows the settings of the active tool or the selected element.

Gas. The particle tool fills a rectangle with gas at a chosen temperature. Walls are impermeable; give them a conductivity κ to exchange heat.

Heat. A heat bath keeps its temperature, a thermal mass warms up and cools down, a heat exchanger is a permeable zone that brings the gas inside to its temperature, a regenerator stores heat along the flow.

Measure. A sensor zone records temperature, pressure, volume and particle count. Bind it to a piston (properties panel) and it follows the piston face and also measures the piston pressure, the right quantity for the work ∮P dV.

Automate. The sequencer at the bottom runs steps: each step sets elements (e.g. drive a piston to a stroke position, switch a heat exchanger) and waits for its transition (time, piston position, sensor value).

Analyse. Every sidebar chart opens large with ⤢: P-V, P-T and T-s diagrams with the work per cycle, zoom, PNG and CSV export. File → Export Data saves all time series.

The examples on the start screen (File → Examples & Recent) show each of these in a working setup. Press ? for the keyboard shortcuts.
